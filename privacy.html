GS DTF — Privacy & Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

GS DTF (also called GS Baseball DTF Studio) turns your designs into print-ready DTF files. All image processing happens on your device.

  • We don't collect data. The images you open are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Photos and camera access are used only to open a design you choose and to save the finished file when you ask.
  • Files you save or share go only where you send them, such as Files, Photos, or another app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Support

Questions or problems with the app? Reach GS Baseball through stingerz-baseball.com.

Tips: use the largest, cleanest copy of your design you have. When saving, choose Save to Files to keep the full 300 DPI PNG.
